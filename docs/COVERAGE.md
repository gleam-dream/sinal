# Sinal design coverage

## Context boundary

- Three bounded contexts own the design: [observation](design/observation/design.typ), [forwarding](design/forwarding/design.typ) and [verification](design/verification/design.typ). The [root design](design/design.typ) holds cross-context foundation, the system at a glance, effects and the end-to-end walkthrough, and the [context map](CONTEXT-MAP.md) declares their relationships.
- Each context's glossary owns its canonical meanings: [observation](design/observation/CONTEXT.typ), [forwarding](design/forwarding/CONTEXT.typ) and [verification](design/verification/CONTEXT.typ). [ADRs](adr/0001-dispatch-through-the-shared-native-telemetry-registry.md) own rationale and evidence summaries.
- `captured` means the named responsibility is specified at its destination. `standard` means an existing conventional artifact needs no package architecture of its own. `out-of-scope` records a deliberate boundary, not a missing inventory row.
